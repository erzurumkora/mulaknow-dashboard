Section 1

About this service

Mula Know Dashboard (“the Dashboard”, “the Service”) is a personal monitoring tool created by the owner of the TikTok account @mulaknow (“the Owner”) to keep track of that account’s own published videos.

The Dashboard connects to TikTok through the official TikTok API, using the application registered as “Mula Know Dashboard”, and retrieves a list of the account’s public videos together with their engagement counts. It exists for one purpose: so the Owner can see, in one place, which videos are live and how many views, likes, comments, and shares each one has received.

The Dashboard is not a public product. It is not marketed, distributed, or made available to other TikTok users, and it provides no service to anyone other than the Owner.

Section 2

What the Dashboard does — and does not do

The Dashboard does

  • List the public videos published by @mulaknow
  • Show the view, like, comment, and share counts for each video
  • Help the Owner confirm that scheduled uploads have been published

The Dashboard does not

  • Read, display, or store the content of any comment

  • Like, follow, message, reply, or otherwise interact with any account
  • Change any account setting, profile detail, or video
  • Access any account other than @mulaknow, or any private content

The Dashboard performs no write operation of any kind against TikTok. Every request it makes is a read request, and it requests only the permissions (scopes) needed to list the Owner’s own videos and read their public counts.
